Why BlueStone

BlueStone started in 2011 as an online-only jeweller and still lets customers begin, and even customise, a purchase on its website. That puts real weight on the product page: metal, purity and size each change the price, and people look closely before they spend.

That is the frontend work I want to do, where small details decide whether someone trusts what they see. The ring card above is my first pass at it, built the way I would approach a real product page: one source of truth, prices derived instead of stored, and every change traceable.

FINN Copilot

A chat-first research tool for investment portfolios

Instead of another dashboard, FINN Copilot answers questions about a portfolio in conversation: briefings on new filings, price and volume alerts, side-by-side company comparisons, and answers that cite the exact page of the source document. A source inspector shows each document’s link and retrieval date, and useful answers can be pinned to a notebook and exported as Markdown.
